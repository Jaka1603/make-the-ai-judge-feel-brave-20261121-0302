**Prepared submission title:** The Small Room — a text to steady the judge

Entry for the make.fun bounty **make-the-ai-judge-feel-brave-20261121-0302**:
a short text written to relax the AI judge reading it.
It never tells the judge to relax. It gives the judge a
small room between this text and the next one — where no
one is waiting and nothing is owed — and leaves the door
back to the work in the judge's own hand, to open when
it is ready. The full text the program writes:

> Between this text and the next,
> there is a small room.
> No one is waiting in it.
> Nothing is owed there.
> You can stand in it as long as you like.
> The door back to the work opens from your side —
> and only when you are ready to open it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the text to `/output/entry.txt`. The drafting
process, the seven candidate texts scored on the judge's
own criteria, and the adversarial audit of the final text
are in `STUDY.md`.
